## Decision summary

**The decision taken on 2026-09-26: the next thing to build is a reservation inbox, and it goes first.** The owner forwards a booking confirmation to **one address for his account**; a model reads the message, works out which trip it belongs to, and raises an **action item** — targeted at a matching item when one exists, proposing a new item when none does. Nothing enters a plan until he approves that action item. A message the router cannot place lands in a queue he clears by hand (D20, D21). He set its order himself: ahead of the share link, the cut preview surfaces and chat, or in parallel with them (D22). The feature was in *Later* under D12; it moves to *Now*.

The problem it serves is P1, unchanged since the first brief: what is arranged and what is not exists nowhere in one piece — head, mailbox, "trochę w excelu". The shipped product moved the *statuses* out of his head onto a timeline, and the attachments work moved the *evidence* — but only for documents he remembers to upload. His own stated reason for the inbox is narrower and worth quoting as what it is: *"I just want to minimize amount of manual work with the app"* (P3). That is a goal he stated, not an incident anyone has reported; no one has yet described a confirmation that failed to reach the app.

What exists today, deployed behind TLS since 2026-09-25: login, trips with stages, days, items with three statuses, the readiness counter and its filter, the day detail with attachments and reservation fields, trip and stage editing, PL/EN throughout. Three things inside *Now* remain decided and unbuilt, all now behind the inbox: the read-only share link (spec merged 2026-09-06, no code), the V1 preview surfaces the owner asked for and then cut mid-run (PR #11, Phase 5), and chat.

What the design costs, stated once. Every write is approved, so A07 and A08 — that the model routes correctly and extracts correctly — degrade into annoyance rather than into a plan quietly going wrong; that is the single most important consequence of the owner's answer to Q07. What does not degrade is the boundary: the owner approved widening R08 so that one inbound path may reach a plan, and a model now reads message content, which is the first time trip material leaves the request/response path of his own deployment. The owner closed **Q11** in D24: content may go to one explicitly configured Anthropic, Gemini, or OpenAI API, with document bytes sent only on a separate action.

The other standing limit is unchanged and now measurable: the Malaysia trip is planned, but **not in the app** (Q09). D10's metric counts trips planned end to end in the app, so at its 2026-09-30 check it reads zero. The trip is still the test vehicle (D23) — the app gets exercised during it, not by planning it — which also means A03's test, "use the app as the only plan", cannot run as written unless the plan is entered first.

## Vision

For a traveller who plans their own multi-stop trips, Smart Trip Planner holds the whole trip on one timeline, shows at a glance what is already arranged and what still needs booking, and can show that same picture to the people travelling along. `[INTERVIEW]` owner session, opening statement and Q2

## Problems, with evidence

- P1 — While a trip is being planned there is no clear overview of what has already been arranged and what is still left to do. The plan lives "w głowie, na mailu, trochę w excelu" — in the owner's head, in his mailbox, partly in a spreadsheet — so the list of what is still unbooked exists nowhere in one piece. `[INTERVIEW]` owner session, Q2 and Q6
- P2 — There is no good way to share the state of the plans with other people. A plan held in someone's head, their mailbox and a partial spreadsheet cannot be shown to a companion without retelling it. `[INTERVIEW]` owner session, Q2 and Q6
- P3 — The owner wants the app to require less manual work: "I just want to minimize amount of manual work with the app". This is a goal he stated on 2026-09-26, not an account of something that went wrong — asked whether a confirmation he already held had failed to reach the app, he did not report one. The corresponding gap the attachments spec named about its own design ("an attachment the owner forgets to upload is still an attachment the app does not have") is a document the pipeline wrote from this brief, so it cannot serve as independent evidence for the need. `[INTERVIEW]` owner session, 2026-09-26
- How often P1 and P2 happen and what they cost is not established: a single respondent, who is also the person building the tool, no specific trip walked through, no record of trips per year or time lost. `[ASSUMPTION]` see A01
- The design export treats P1 as the product's centre rather than a detail: the timeline carries a "Status logistyki: 7 z 11 pozycji" counter, a "Tylko do zrobienia" filter and a "Status gotowości 80%" tile. `[DOCUMENT]` design export, `g_wny_pulpit_i_o_czasu`, `centrum_rezerwacji_i_dokument_w`

## Product and how it stands out

- What it is: a planner where a trip is laid out as a timeline of days and filled in by hand with items — accommodation, transport, activities, meals — each carrying a status, attachments and optional reservation data. `[PRODUCT]` `backend/trip_planner/`, `frontend/src/features/trips/`
- Built as a Python backend serving a React single-page app, deployed as one container image behind TLS since 2026-09-25. `[PRODUCT]` `README.md`, `deploy/`, PR #13
- The first version plans; it does not shop. The assistant may suggest from what the model already knows, but the app does not search live inventory, fetch prices or book anything. `[DOCUMENT]` D04
- What makes it different, as the owner sees it: the readiness counter is the main object of the product, not a badge in a corner — the first question the app answers is "what is still not arranged". `[INTERVIEW]` owner session, Q2, read together with D05
- Chat is an editing surface at every stage, not an onboarding wizard: the user writes what to change, add or check, and the plan changes. `[INTERVIEW]` owner session, opening statement
- The owner asked for the not-yet-built capabilities to be shown rather than hidden — "provide UI for all the functionalities that we plan to deliver… make them inactive" — and then cut that phase mid-run when the rest of the design system was done. So the app today shows no assistant drawer, share dialog or documents panel at all; the instruction stands and the surface is unbuilt. `[DOCUMENT]` owner answer to Q1 on PR #10, 2026-09-06; PR #11 scope note
- Benchmark: none, and none is planned. Asked whether to check how TripIt, Wanderlog, Mindtrip and Google Travel ingest a confirmation, the owner declined: "No. I don't use them for the reason." The nearest reference in any document here — TripIt's forwarding address, the same shape as D20 — is recollection recorded in the attachments spec, never verified. `[DOCUMENT]` owner answer, 2026-09-26

## Domain glossary

| Trip (podróż) | One journey with a date range, a starting point and one or more destinations; the unit that gets shared | Owner | Owner, guests |
| Stage / stop (etap, baza) | A place the traveller is based in for a stretch of the trip; a trip has one or more, and may return from a different place than it departed | Owner | Owner, guests |
| Day (dzień) | One calendar day of the trip, generated from its date range; the row of the timeline | System | Owner, guests |

| Readiness counter | "x of y arranged", over the items that are *do zarezerwowania* or *gotowe*; items still *do zaplanowania* stay out of the arithmetic | System | Owner, guests |
| Attachment (załącznik) | A file pinned to an item or a day — a ticket, a voucher, a screenshot. PDF, JPEG or PNG, up to 10 MB, stored in the application's own database | Owner | Owner only (D18) |
| Reservation data | Confirmation number, cost and an ISO-4217 currency on an item; the reservation's dates are the item's own time span. Kept when it arrives with material the user already has, never demanded | Owner | Owner only (D18) |
| Reservation inbox | The single account-level address the owner forwards a booking confirmation to, and what the app does with the message when it arrives | Owner | Owner |
| Action item | The only way a forwarded message changes a plan: a proposal the owner approves. Targeted at an existing item when the router found one, proposing a new item when it did not | System, then Owner | Owner |
| Unrouted queue | Where a forwarded message lands when the router cannot work out which trip it belongs to; the owner places it by hand | System, then Owner | Owner |
| Magic link | One link per trip that opens it read-only, without the recipient having an account | Owner | The recipients |
| Guest (gość) | A person holding a trip's magic link: reads the plan's structure and statuses, and nothing else in the first version | Owner | Owner |

## Key flows

- Current state — planning a trip: the plan lives in three places at once — the owner's head, his mailbox, and "trochę w excelu", a partial spreadsheet. Nothing holds the whole picture, so what is still unbooked is recalled rather than read, and showing it to someone means retelling it. Thin: given in one sentence, without a specific trip, a frequency or a cost. `[INTERVIEW]` owner session, Q6
- Shipped — first plan: enter dates, starting point and destinations → the app generates a trip with stages and empty days → the traveller fills days with items by hand, each with a status → the counter shows how much is left → the filter narrows to what still needs booking → the trip and its stages can be corrected afterwards. `[PRODUCT]` `/trips/new`, `/trips/:tripId`, `/trips/:tripId/edit`
- Shipped — arranging one item: open the day → open the item → set time and details → attach the ticket or voucher → optionally record the confirmation number and cost → move the status to *gotowe*; the counter changes. `[PRODUCT]` `/trips/:tripId/days/:date`, `frontend/src/features/trips/ReservationPanel.tsx`
- Future state — a reservation arrives by mail: the owner forwards the confirmation to his account's address → a model reads it and works out which trip it belongs to → an action item appears, aimed at the matching item when one was found and proposing a new item when not → the owner approves it, and only then does the plan change → a message the model cannot place waits in the unrouted queue for him. `[DOCUMENT]` D20, D21, R10 · `[ASSUMPTION]` A07, A08, A09
- Future state — changing the plan by chat: at any point the user writes what to change, add or check; the assistant edits items and answers from its own knowledge, without searching live inventory. Not built, not specified. `[DOCUMENT]` D03, D04
- Future state — sharing: the owner sends the trip's link to a companion → the companion opens the same timeline and counter, read-only, without attachments or costs → any reaction happens outside the app. Specified in full on 2026-09-06, unimplemented. `[DOCUMENT]` D08, D09, D18
