## Brand & Style

The design system projects effortless mastery, curated luxury, and calm intelligence for modern, discerning travelers. It moves away from chaotic booking portals toward an editorial, high-trust travel atelier.

### Design Movement & Aesthetic
- **Contemporary Minimalist Atelier:** Architectural structure defined by generous whitespace, hairline borders, and pure tonal contrast.
- **Atmospheric Depth:** Frosted glass headers and side panels ground content over clean, warm slate backdrops.
- **Target Audience:** Modern explorers, business-leisure travelers, and remote workers who prioritize structure, clarity, and instant context when managing complex cross-city itineraries.
- **Emotional Intent:** Reassurance, inspiring precision, and absolute tranquility amidst the logistical frenzy of travel.

## Colors

The palette grounds the interface in deep ocean stability, punctuated by warm golden-hour warmth and functional semantic signals.

### Functional Roles
- **Primary (`#0F3F6D`):** Deep oceanic sapphire. Used for core structural navigation, dominant CTA states, selected tabs, and foundational headers.
- **Secondary (`#EA580C`):** Terracotta coral. Reserved for AI-driven insights, live itinerary alerts, active smart-chips, and focal interaction points.
- **Tertiary / Success (`#10B981`):** Emerald green for confirmed reservations, completed vouchers, and paid statuses (*Zarezerwowane / Potwierdzone*).
- **Warning / Pending (`#F59E0B`):** Warm amber for pending bookings, deadlines, and draft action items (*W trakcie rezerwacji*).
- **Draft / Inactive (`#64748B`):** Muted slate for exploratory draft notes, unsaved plans, and secondary badges (*Szkic*).
- **Neutral Canvas:** `#F8FAFC` base application canvas with `#FFFFFF` elevated card containers, framed by `#E2E8F0` structural hair lines.
- **Text & Content Hierarchy:** `#0F172A` (Headlines & primary reading), `#334155` (Subheads & metadata labels), `#64748B` (Secondary captions & disabled states).

## Typography

The typography leverages **Plus Jakarta Sans** across all roles to achieve a blend of contemporary geometric precision with sculpted humanistic terminals.

### Treatment & Rhythm
- Polish diacritics (`ą, ć, ę, ł, ń, ó, ś, ź, ż`) maintain full optical balance and line-height integrity across all font weights.
- Itinerary headings use tight negative tracking (`-0.015em` to `-0.03em`) for a premium editorial character.
- Small labels, chips, and flight codes feature subtle positive tracking (`+0.015em` to `+0.025em`) to retain legibility at glance speeds.
- Currency markers, time milestones, and flight legs maintain identical baseline alignment using consistent height scaling.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model is a hybrid split-surface workspace calibrated for trip planning:
- **Desktop (1280px+):** A 12-column dynamic fluid layout flanked by a persistent or collapsible travel assistant drawer (`sidebar-width: 22rem` / 352px). The central itinerary uses an 8-column canvas paired with a 4-column contextual meta and document dock.
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px):** 8-column grid with full-width itinerary timeline cards. The AI drawer transforms into an overlay sheet triggered via action chips.
- **Mobile (<768px):** Single-column stacked workflow. Horizontal tab bars switch views between *Plan Podróży (Timeline)*, *Dokumenty (Vault)*, and *Asystent AI (Chat)*.

### Spatial Cadence
- Cards follow a strict 8pt rhythm, reserving `1.5rem` (24px) for outer card padding and `0.75rem` (12px) for related group clusters (e.g., flight timeline nodes to transfer badges).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses a crisp layered approach: hairline boundaries paired with diffused ambient light rather than aggressive dark drop shadows.

### Elevation Levels
- **Base Canvas (`elevation-0`):** `#F8FAFC`, non-elevated flat plane.
- **Resting Cards (`elevation-1`):** Pure `#FFFFFF` surface with a 1px border (`#E2E8F0`) and a soft ambient shadow: `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 63, 109, 0.04), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(15, 63, 109, 0.02)`.
- **Active / Hover State (`elevation-2`):** Elevates slightly on hover for interactive timeline items: `0 10px 15px -3px rgba(15, 63, 109, 0.06), 0 4px 6px -4px rgba(15, 63, 109, 0.03)` with border shifting to `#CBD5E1`.
- **Drawer / Floating Modals (`elevation-3`):** AI conversational drawer and document quick-look sheets: `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)` over a frosted backdrop (`backdrop-blur: 12px`, `#0F172A` at 20% opacity).
- **Floating Operational Overlays:** Tab headers and sticky date anchors leverage `backdrop-filter: blur(8px)` with an 85% alpha white background (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)`).

## Shapes

The design system employs **Level 2 (Rounded)** shape geometry, pairing generous radii with structural rigor:
- **Base UI Elements (`rounded` / 0.5rem - 8px):** Form fields, action buttons, timeline nodes, and document badges.
- **Containers & Surfaces (`rounded-lg` / 1rem - 16px):** Trip segment cards, itinerary day modules, and upload dropzones.
- **Drawers & Floating Sheets (`rounded-xl` / 1.5rem - 24px):** Chat popovers, side drawers, and destination summary banners.
- **Pills (`rounded-full`):** Status chips (*Zarezerwowane*), day selector pills, and AI prompt suggestion tokens.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Solid `#0F3F6D` background, `#FFFFFF` text, `0.5rem` radius, 40px height for standard actions. Hover: `#0C3257` with subtle scale transition.
- **AI Action / Accent:** Subtle gradient or solid `#EA580C` background with `#FFFFFF` text for AI generation tools (*"Zaplanuj z AI"*).
- **Secondary / Ghost:** Transparent background, `#0F3F6D` text with 1px hairline `#E2E8F0` border. Hover: `#F1F5F9`.

### Status Chips & Badges
- **Confirmed (*Zarezerwowane*):** `#ECFDF5` background, `#059669` text, 1px `#A7F3D0` border, `rounded-full`, accompanied by a 6px solid emerald dot.
- **In Progress (*W trakcie rezerwacji*):** `#FFFBEB` background, `#D97706` text, 1px `#FDE68A` border.
- **Draft (*Szkic / Planowane*):** `#F1F5F9` background, `#475569` text, 1px `#E2E8F0` border.
- **AI Sparkle Tag:** `#FFF7ED` background, `#C2410C` text, paired with a small geometric sparkle icon.

### Timeline Itinerary Card
- White `#FFFFFF` card body with a 1px `#E2E8F0` border and `1rem` radius.
- Left-aligned connected line anchor (1.5px solid `#E2E8F0`) with timeline status indicator dot.
- Content zone: Time stamp in bold slate (`#0F172A`), category icon (Flight, Hotel, Rail, Activity), title, confirmation number chip, and document preview pills.

### Conversational AI Assistant Drawer
- Anchored to the right viewport edge, utilizing a frosted background (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.95)` with `12px` blur).
- Top bar with trip context picker (*np. "Tokio & Kioto - Dzień 3"*).
- Assistant chat bubbles styled in `#F8FAFC` with a 1px border; user queries right-aligned in `#0F3F6D` with white text.
- Integrated quick prompt suggestions displayed as horizontal scrolling chips (*"Dodaj rezerwację kolacji", "Sprawdź dojazd pociągiem"*).

### Document Upload Dropzone
- Border: 1.5px dashed `#CBD5E1`, background: `#F8FAFC` transitioning to `#F1F5F9` on drag-over.
- Center icon with upload graphic, primary prompt (*"Przeciągnij potwierdzenie PDF lub rezerwację"*), supporting microcopy (*"Obsługuje PDF, Apple Wallet, JPG do 25MB"*).

### Input Fields & Date Pickers
- Border: 1px `#CBD5E1`, background: `#FFFFFF`, active outline: 2px solid `#0F3F6D` with `0.5rem` corner radius.
- Date Range Picker: Dual calendar surface with deep oceanic range fill (`#0F3F6D` at 10% opacity) and solid `#0F3F6D` start/end date anchors.
