# D12 — No feature is permanently excluded; everything outside the first version is "later"
- Date, owner: 2026-09-05, Michal Klosinski
- Context and the options weighed: Non-goals are the section that later protects a project from scope creep, and other skills in this pipeline treat them as a contract. The owner was asked twice, and the two answers disagreed.
- Decision and why: "Wszystkie opcje można dodać potem, żadna nie musi być teraz, żadnej nie wykluczam." In the second round the owner accepted an agent-proposed list with four hard exclusions (booking and payments, live prices, cost splitting, public plans); when that contradiction was put back to him he chose "nic nie wykluczam". This record supersedes that acceptance.
- Consequences, and what would make us revisit it: N01 in the brief is deliberately empty of exclusions. The boundary of the first version is the *Now* list in Scope, and nothing else. Reviewers cannot appeal to non-goals to stop scope creep in this project.
- Required path to change: A superseding record naming the feature actually being ruled out, and why.
