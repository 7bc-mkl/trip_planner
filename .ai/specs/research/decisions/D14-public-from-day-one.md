# D14 — The application is on the public internet from day one, so authentication is a must-have
- Date, owner: 2026-09-05, Michal Klosinski
- Context and the options weighed: The review of the draft brief objected that login and sharing sat in the first version only to serve assumption A01 ("friends have this problem"), which has no evidence, while the only confirmed user is the owner on his own machine.
- Decision and why: "To będzie stało w internecie od dnia 1 — autoryzacja to must have." Authentication is therefore not a feature for future users; it is a consequence of the deployment model.
- Consequences, and what would make us revisit it: R08 in the brief: no screen showing a plan may be reachable without either an owner session or a trip's magic link. The cost of authentication is inside the twenty-five days to 2026-09-30, which is why assumption A05 names it explicitly.
- Required path to change: A superseding record, or a decision to keep the first version off the public internet.
