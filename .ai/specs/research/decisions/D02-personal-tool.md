# D02 — A personal tool: no paying customer, no revenue goal
- Date, owner: 2026-09-05, Michal Klosinski
- Context and the options weighed: Asked who the product is for: a tool for the owner and his circle, or a product for a market with someone eventually paying.
- Decision and why: "Ja i znajomi" — the owner and his friends, with no paying customer. Refined in the fourth round to the owner alone first (see D15).
- Consequences, and what would make us revisit it: There is no business goal in the brief and no user-count target; the primary metric is the owner's own use. Sections about pricing, acquisition and monetisation are absent by decision, not by omission.
- Required path to change: A superseding record, e.g. if the owner decides to open it to others and charge.
