# D09 — One editor per trip; a guest reads only
- Date, owner: 2026-09-05, Michal Klosinski
- Context and the options weighed: Asked whether a trip has one owner or several people editing it. The first answer was "najpierw główny edytor, i podgląd, z ew. możliwością skomentowania, wysłania sugestii" — with commenting as an optional extra, which the draft brief wrongly promoted to committed scope.
- Decision and why: One editor — the owner. A guest reads and nothing more: "read only w v1". Comments and suggestions move to the *Later* list.
- Consequences, and what would make us revisit it: R06 in the brief. Assumption A06 (a read-only link is enough for companions) stays open and is tested during the Malaysia trip.
- Required path to change: A superseding record; comments, suggestions or co-editing would each need one.
