# D07 — Costs are stored when they arrive with material the user already has
- Date, owner: 2026-09-05, Michal Klosinski
- Context and the options weighed: The design export puts budget and cost figures on almost every screen. The question was whether the first version tracks money at all, given that the owner's stated problem is about what is arranged, not about what it costs.
- Decision and why: "Jeżeli informacja będzie dostępna (np. z uploadu rezerwacji) to warto ją trzymać; w przyszłości rozliczanie może być istotną funkcją."
- Consequences, and what would make us revisit it: R04 in the brief: cost and reservation data are kept when they come with material, and never demanded from the user. Cost accounting between participants is on the *Later* list. Whether the first version handles more than one currency is left open as Q04.
- Required path to change: A superseding record; making cost entry a first-class feature would need one.
