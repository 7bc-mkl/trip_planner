What this screen must never do

  • Require a reservation field to save, attach, or reach Done.
  • Block on a modal, or interrupt the flow.
  • Open this disclosure by itself, ever — the user opens it.
  • Show any "incomplete" badge, count, prompt or reminder for an empty field.
  • Let reservation data move the readiness counter.

These are R04's "never demanded" written as invariants — Phase 3 steps 3, 5 and 6 test each one.
