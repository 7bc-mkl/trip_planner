What this screen must never do

  • Require a reservation field to save, attach, or reach Done.
  • Block on a modal, or interrupt the flow.
  • Auto-expand more than once per item.
  • Show any "incomplete" badge, count, or reminder for an empty field.
  • Let reservation data move the readiness counter.

These are R04's "never demanded", written as invariants — Phase 3 steps 4 and 5 test each one.
